Medhunt Privacy Notice

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Information Medhunt processes

Medhunt can read professional information visible on supported candidate and healthcare-provider pages. This may include a person's name, location, role, employer, education, specialty, licenses, profile URL, and other professional profile information.

After an authorized recruiter selects a candidate and starts an action, Medhunt may also process professional email addresses and phone numbers returned by enrichment services, as well as resumes or public-profile documents the recruiter chooses to capture.

For account access, Medhunt processes the recruiter's work email address and an opaque, limited login session.

When information leaves the browser

Page detection and profile review remain in the browser. Selected candidate information is transmitted to the Medhunt backend only when the recruiter explicitly starts contact lookup, profile import, or resume capture.

How information is used

Information is used only to support recruiting workflows requested by the recruiter: creating candidate records, finding professional contact details, producing or attaching resumes, preventing contact with suppressed recipients, and synchronizing records with the organization's configured recruiting system.

Medhunt does not sell candidate information, use it for advertising, or send outreach automatically.

Service providers and storage

Selected information may be processed by contracted hosting, database, enrichment, document-storage, authentication, and recruiting-system providers solely to operate these features. Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Access to protected backend functions requires an authenticated recruiter session.

Retention and deletion

Candidate records and documents are retained according to the deploying organization's recruiting and legal-retention requirements. Recruiters should contact their organization administrator to correct or delete a candidate record and to handle privacy or opt-out requests.

Authorized use

Recruiters must use Medhunt only for information their organization is authorized to process and must comply with applicable platform terms, privacy requirements, and do-not-contact obligations.
